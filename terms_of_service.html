3. App Description

Audio Editor is a mobile application for editing audio and video files. Features include:

  • Mute audio or video, including a selected sub-range
  • Auto Boost, which increases volume up to 200%
  • Tone (pitch) change and equalizer
  • Mix tracks, to add audio to a video or to another audio file
  • Trim audio or video
  • Change playback speed of audio
  • Extract audio from video, with output in M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, Opus, 3GP or AMR, and adjustable bitrate, sample rate and channels

All processing occurs on-device.
